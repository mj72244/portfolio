# Sample Alignment

Module 1: Pain Management in Sickle Cell Crisis and Renal Colic (Kidney Stones)

## Learning Objectives:

1. Course Learning Outcome (CLO) A: Apply Maryland Medical Protocols (MIEMSS, 2025) to select and administer safe, evidence-based analgesic interventions in prehospital special-case pain scenarios while minimizing risks such as respiratory depression or hypotension.

   * Unit Objective 1: Identify key indicators for opioid vs. non-opioid analgesia in sickle cell crisis and renal colic presentations, including patient assessment using pain scales and vital sign considerations.
2. Course Learning Outcome (CLO) B: Demonstrate ethical decision-making in pain management, prioritizing patient comfort, equity, and protocol adherence across diverse prehospital scenarios.

   * Unit Objective 2: Differentiate pain management approaches for sickle cell crisis (often requiring aggressive multimodal therapy) versus renal colic (often responsive to NSAIDs or ketorolac with hydration considerations)

## Assessment Strategies

* Pre-Module Knowledge Check (Formative, Low-Stakes)

Aligned Learning Activity: Interactive multiple choice embedded in the microlearning module. Reviews base line knowledge of Maryland Medical Protocols (2025) pain management basics including drug and non-drug interventions and contraindications.

Aligned Learning Objective: Identify core principles in standard prehospital pain management scenarios (CLO alignment: apply safe, evidence based analgesic interventions in accordance with protocol)

* Final Knowledge Application (Summative)

Aligned Learning Activity: End of module quiz with scenario-based questions, including multiple choice questions with a focus on prioritization.

Aligned Learning Activity: Demonstrate application of protocols to special case pain management, ensuring consistent application in practice (CLO alignment: demonstrate ethical decision making, prioritizing patient comfort and outcomes).
