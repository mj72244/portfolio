Target Audience

## Background and Prior Knowledge

All hold current Maryland paramedic licensure. Experience varies widely:

* Newly licensed (0-2 years): recent initial entry training, limited exposure to less common cases
* Mid-career (3-10 years): routine protocol knowledge
* Veterans (10+ years): extensive field experience, may benefit from targeted updates on current research and best practices to ensure continued excellence

They have foundational knowledge of Maryland protocols for common emergencies, Advanced Life Support (ALS) skills including intravenous access, intraosseous access, intubation, and additional credentials including Advanced Cardiac Life Support (ACLS), Pediatric Advanced Life Support (PALS), and Prehospital Trauma Life Support (PHTLS). Biennial licensure renewal requires continuing education including protocol updates.

The course stems from discussions with clinicians across various forums, including previous continuing education courses, where they expressed a desire for greater confidence in managing less common pain management scenarios.

### Roles and Assignments

Participants include:

* Paramedics on transport units (ALS ambulances), providing direct patient care on routine and high-volume calls.
* EMS officers/company officers offering on-scene judgment, advice, and supervision.
* Department leadership who oversee compliance, quality assurance, and training but rarely deliver hands-on care.

### Employment Status

Most students are career (full-time, paid) fire department employees. The course also includes paramedic volunteers, common in combination career/volunteer systems, who provide ALS care on a part-time basis with potentially less frequent field exposure.

## Other Relevant Information

The course targets those fulfilling continuing education requirements for National Registry of EMTs (NREMT) and Maryland Institute of EMS Systems (MIEMSS) paramedic certification and licensure. The course leverages the department’s Moodle learning management system to deliver flexible, accessible delivery that accommodates a wide range of technology comfort levels. Students will be able to access the course on the devices that they prefer, from mobile devices to desktop computers. Moodle is the platform for all the department’s training with a dedicated team to support the platform and students.

Moodle is committed “to be fully accessible and usable for all users regardless of ability” (Moodle, 2025). Moodle conforms to several industry and government standards, and supports assistive technologies, including screen-readers, promoting access for all learners.
