# Course Type

## Problem Solving Course

In this course learners engage with realistic scenario-based challenges to analyze situations, apply Maryland Medical Protocols, make evidence-based decisions, and evaluate outcomes. Learners will build skills to handle uncommon prehospital pain management scenarios. Content is delivered in short interactive case simulations such as sickle cell crisis, renal colic, and palliative care with DNR/MOLST orders. Branching scenarios and knowledge checks require learners too solve problems drawn from real life scenarios.

This is the most suitable course to address the identified knowledge gap: suboptimal pain management in special case prehospital scenarios due to limited exposure and education. Because paramedics encounter these situations infrequently their lack of confidence leads to increased patient suffering and ethical issues.

A problem-solving approach mirrors real-world EMS decision making and bridges experience gaps among diverse learners (Newly certified vs. veterans, active clinicians vs. supervisors). It aligns with adult learning principles by focusing on practical application and immediate relevance, building confidence in rare but high-stakes scenarios.
