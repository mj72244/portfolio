# Course Modality

The modality for this course is asynchronous online. It is self-paced via Moodle mobile modules. Learners complete 45 minutes of microlearning modules at their convenience, with no scheduled live sessions, real-time interaction, or location requirements. Features include interactive simulations, video/audio narration, progress tracking, and quizzes.

This modality is appropriate for our target learners: busy paramedics. Asynchronous delivery eliminates barriers including travel, fixed class times, and shift conflicts.
