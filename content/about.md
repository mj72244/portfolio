## About the Learning Design and Technology Master's Degree at UMGC

Blending theory, research, and hands-on practice to equip learners with the expertise to design, analyze, and deliver technology-enhanced learning experiences.

I’m thrilled to build on my experience as an emergency services instructor, sharpening my skills as an instructional designer and educator to create impactful, technology-enhanced learning experiences.

Explore my journey through the navigation above. See below for more about this program or visit [UMGC](https://www.umgc.edu/online-degrees/masters/learning-design-technology).

The program requires a total of 30 credits:

Core Courses  

* Learning Theories and Learner Analysis in Learning Design (3 Credits, LDTC 600)
* Instructional Design Models to Inform Learning Design (3 Credits, LDTC 605)
* Instructional Media in Learning Design (3 Credits, LDTC 610)
* UX and UI Design in Course Development (3 Credits, LDTC 615)
* Next Generation Design: Emerging Technology, Gamification, and AI in Learning Design (3 Credits, LDTC 620)
* Analytics and Evaluation in Learning Design (3 Credits, LDTC 625)
* Collaboration and Design Thinking in Learning Design (3 Credits, LDTC 630)
* Project Management for Learning Design (3 Credits, LDTC 640)
* Instructional Strategy and Practice in Online Learning Environments (3 Credits, LDTC 650)

Capstone Course

* Learning Design Capstone (3 Credits, LDTC 690)
