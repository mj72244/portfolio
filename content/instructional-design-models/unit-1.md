# Instructional Design - Ethical Considerations

Ethical consideration in instructional design is a topic that resonates for me for several reasons. First Devlin Peck's comments regarding designing courses just because we're asked. Also a recent reel on Instagram by paramedicsophie (<https://www.instagram.com/reel/DTSqsb4kfSF/?igsh=MWVhYmRsMncxM3A4Zg==>) recently about how pointless checkbox training is destroying morale in EMS. Training that does not make us safer, smarted, or better but instead communicates that our time and attention are not valuable.

It resonates for me because having previously managed paramedic continuing education for my department I have felt the pressure to churn out course content to meet training requirements. At the same time I've certianly been on the otherside what has been clearly been "check the box" mandatory education.

HSI has a white paper that discusses recomendations for better instructional design in mandatory training to move beyond minimal compliance: <https://hsi.com/resources/why-compliance-training-is-more-than-a-checkbox>

Another resource is the book: Applied Ethics for Instructional Design and Technology available at: <https://edtechbooks.org/applied_ethics_idt> that I have just started to read. I look forward to more deeply investigating the topic, especially in the light that paramedicsophie shines on the topic.
