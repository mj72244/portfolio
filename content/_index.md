## Biography

I am a passionate firefighter/paramedic and emergency services instructor pursuing a Masters in Learning Design and Technology. With over a decade of experience instructing, mentoring, and precepting, I am committed to designing and delivering engaging, relevant training for today’s and tomorrow’s first responders.

My experience responding to emergencies, combined with my time in my department’s training bureau, has underscored the critical need for timely and effective training. As first responders face increasingly complex challenges, I am passionate about creating learning solutions to equip them for success.
