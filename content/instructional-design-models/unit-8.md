# Learning Theories Leveraged

The primary learning theory driving the design of the learning activities in this minicourse is constructivism, complemented by principles of andragogy (adult learning theory). This combination is particularly effective for a continuing education mini-course targeting practicing paramedics, as it supports active knowledge construction in realistic, scenario-based contexts while respecting learners' prior expertise and need for practical relevance.

Constructivism drives the interactive core of the simulations and reflections, while andragogy shapes learner-centered, practical, and respectful approach to delivery.  This blended approach promotes confidence in handling complex pain management cases, addresses knowledge gaps in special scenarios, and promotes professional growth for busy paramedics ranging from novice to veteran, and across a variety of organizational roles.
